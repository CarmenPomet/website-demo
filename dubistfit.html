du bist fit! [2025]

KUG, Graz (AT)
Carmen Pomet & Diego Piñera
▶ Full Version



du bist fit! [2025] is a performative intervention that transforms a concert room into a dynamic playground, where movement and participation become tools for reimagining our social and spatial relationships. The performance rethinks public space and shared activity by inviting audiences to engage directly, either by participating in or observing a structured workout. Blending sport, play, and artistic intervention, the piece opens a space for affective engagement and collective interaction.
