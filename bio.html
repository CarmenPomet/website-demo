My name is Carmen Pomet. I am a multidisciplinary, queer artist working across sound, performance, and installation. I have a background in political science, composition and philosophy and I am currently completing a Master’s degree in Computer Music and Sound Art at the University of Music and Performing Arts Graz.

My work unfolds between live performance, interactive installations, and time-based media, often creating situations in which audiences become part of the piece. I am interested in how sound, space, and bodies interact, and how environments can be shaped collectively in real time.

Working with hybrid systems that connect physical and digital elements, I develop performative and spatial setups using sound processing, sensors, and custom-built electronics. These systems respond to presence and movement, generating evolving environments where sound and space are continuously in flux.

At the core of my practice are questions of perception, agency and social relations—how we experience, construct and negotiate the spaces we share.
