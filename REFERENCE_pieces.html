du bist fit! [2025]


KUG, Graz (AT)
Carmen Pomet & Diego Piñera

Link to YouTube
du bist fit is an performative intervention that transforms a concert room into a dynamic playground, where movement and participation become tools for reimagining our social and spatial relationships. The public is invited to join or observe a structured workout. The performance challenges traditional perceptions of public space and shared activity. By merging sport, play, and artistic intervention we create a space for affective engagement, community interaction and reimagination of the everyday through the body, physicality and collectivity.
